Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer complimentary DHL/FedEx Express VIP delivery worldwide on all orders exceeding $100. Deliveries within North America and Europe typically arrive within 2–4 business days. All shipments are fully insured and tracked.
We accept returns or exchanges on all unwashed, unworn garments with original tags intact within 30 days of receipt. We provide a pre-paid courier return label directly via our portal or email request.
Our t-shirts, hoodies, and outerwear are designed with an intentional relaxed, boxy architectural cut. If you prefer the contemporary oversized drape seen in our editorial lookbooks, select your normal size. For a standard tailored fit, we recommend sizing down one size.
To preserve the heavy handfeel, rich dye depth, and architectural shape of our 300+ GSM cottons, machine wash cold (30°C or lower) inside out with gentle detergent. Lay flat to air dry; avoid high-heat tumble drying.
